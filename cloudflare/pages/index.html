Market Regime Algorithm

Formula
score  =  Σi  tier(xi) × wi
xicurrent reading of indicator i
tier(xi)signed extremeness: ±1 mild, ±2 strong, ±3 extreme
wiquality weight (see table →)
score > 0bottom / buy bias
score < 0top / caution bias

Regime alerts

Get a push notification when the Poor2Rich market regime moves into a band you pick.

A new band has to hold for two checks in a row (about 30–60 min) before an alert goes out, and the same band isn't announced twice within 12 hours. Alerts are an early-warning gauge, not trading advice.
